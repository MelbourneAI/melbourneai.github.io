Privacy Policy

Effective date: 8 September 2026 · Last updated: 15 September 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how MelbourneAIQbix ("the Company", "we", "us") collects, uses, and protects information through the Transport Request Assistant — an internal WhatsApp-based system that lets employees request company transport and lets the transport team manage vehicle allocation and third-party vendor bidding.

This service is provided for use by employees and approved transport vendors of MelbourneAIQbix only. It is not a consumer-facing product and is not available to the general public.

1. Information we collect

  • Employee identity data: name, employee code, department, assigned branch/location, and WhatsApp phone number, provided by your employer's HR/transport administration when your account is set up.
  • Messages you send: the text and voice notes you send to the WhatsApp number for this service, and automated transcriptions of voice notes.
  • Travel request details: pickup and destination locations, requested date and time, number of passengers, and any vehicle preferences you provide.
  • Request history and status: matched trips, vendor quotations (including declined/expired quotes), approval or rejection decisions, and the reasoning recorded for each.
  • Vendor data: for approved transport vendors, business name, WhatsApp contact number, service area, vehicle types, rate information, and the quote messages vendors send to this service.

2. How we use this information

  • To understand and process your transport requests, including matching you with existing trips or sourcing a vehicle from an approved vendor.
  • To route requests to the transport team for review and approval.
  • To notify you of the status of your request (submitted, approved, rejected, or needing more information).
  • To maintain an audit trail of requests, vendor quotes, and approval decisions, for accountability and dispute resolution.
  • To measure and improve service quality (for example, how accurately requests are understood).

3. Automated processing

Messages you send are processed by an AI system to extract structured details (such as destination and travel date) from your natural-language message. Vendor quote messages are read the same way to suggest the quoted price and vehicle, which a member of the transport team then checks and records. This AI system never makes decisions about vehicle capacity, availability, or request approval. Requests that only take a free seat on an existing trip are approved automatically by a fixed, written company rule, not by AI. Every other request, including any that requires hiring a vehicle, is approved by a member of the transport team. Voice notes are automatically transcribed to text so they can be processed the same way as typed messages.

4. Who we share information with

  • The transport team at MelbourneAIQbix, who review and approve/reject requests.
  • Approved transport vendors, who receive only the trip details necessary to provide a quote (pickup/destination, date, time, vehicle type, passenger count) — not your full message history.
  • Service providers that operate the underlying technology: WhatsApp/Meta (for message delivery), our AI language-processing provider, and our voice-transcription provider — each processes data solely to provide their respective service to us, under their own data-processing terms.

We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with any third party for their own marketing purposes.

5. Data retention

Request history, vendor quotes, and approval decisions are retained for as long as needed to maintain an accurate audit trail of company transport activity, and in line with MelbourneAIQbix's internal records-retention policy. Conversational session state (the AI's short-term memory of an in-progress conversation) is automatically cleared after a short period of inactivity.

6. Data security

Access to request data is restricted to authenticated members of the transport team. Data is stored in access-controlled systems and transmitted over encrypted connections.

7. Your rights

As an employee or vendor, you may ask what information is held about you, request a correction, request deletion of your data (subject to our obligation to keep audit records of transport decisions), or raise a concern about how your data is used, by contacting us using the details below.
